HOW IT WORKS

Three seconds to
clean text

No complicated setup, no voice training, no dashboard to learn.

01

Hold or Double-Tap

Press and hold Right Alt (or double-tap for hands-free mode). A subtle floating soundwave HUD tells you Voice is listening.

02

Say It Out Loud

Talk the way you naturally would. Ramble, pause, self-correct, or think out loud. Whisper and Vosk live preview keep up in real time.

03

Let Go

Release the hotkey. Polished, punctuated, formatted text lands directly where your cursor was blinking.
